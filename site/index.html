01

A wallet in your browser.

zebgen is a full-fledged non-custodial browser wallet, serving Algorand and Monero. Your private keys never leave your browser.

02

Simple yet powerful.

Transfer ALGO or XMR, choose your node, and review before signing. Everyday wallet essentials.

03

Tailor-made for HMD2V applications

Beyond facilitating simple transactions zebgen brings application-specific flows into the wallet, starting with LeakSwap.

02 / TAILOR-MADE FOR LeakSwap

More than a connection.

LeakSwap is zebgen's first tailored application. Exchange ALGO and XMR with a cross-chain atomic-swap protocol, with approvals and swap-specific key handling built into the wallet.

Anchored in Algorand's instant finality and ~2.7s blocktime, LeakSwap is the fastest non-custodial XMR cross-chain swap platform out there.

More applications coming.

TESTER RELEASE

Start with test funds.

zebgen is under active development. Use disposable test wallets and test networks. Keep recovery material backed up before experimenting.

Need funds for testing? Check out: Algorand Testnet faucet (Lora) or Monero Stagenet faucet (Nodely).

Nodes are graciously hosted by Nodely.io, but you are free to use your own.

Tester builds · Manual installation

  1. Download & unzip

    Choose the Chrome archive from the release assets and extract it into a folder you will keep.

  2. Open Extensions

    Visit chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.

  3. Load zebgen

    Select Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder containing the manifest.

  1. Download & unzip

    Choose the Firefox archive from the release assets and extract it.

  2. Open debugging

    Visit about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.

  3. Load the temporary add-on

    Select Load Temporary Add-on and choose manifest.json in the extracted folder. Temporary add-ons are removed when Firefox restarts.
